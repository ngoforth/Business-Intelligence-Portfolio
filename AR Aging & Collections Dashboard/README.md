# AR Aging & Collections Dashboard

ar-aging-dashboard.png

## Project Summary

Developed an Accounts Receivable (AR) Aging and Collections dashboard to provide stakeholders with visibility into outstanding invoices, customer payments, aging balances, and collection performance.

The solution integrated financial data from Spectrum and Microsoft Dynamics 365 into Snowflake, where it was transformed and modeled for reporting in Power BI. The dashboard provided a centralized view of AR activity, helping teams monitor invoice status, payment trends, and outstanding balances while supporting collection efforts and financial decision-making.

## Project Overview

Developed a Power BI Accounts Receivable dashboard that consolidated invoice, payment, and aging information into a centralized reporting solution.

Data from Spectrum and Microsoft Dynamics 365 was integrated into Snowflake and transformed into a reporting-ready dataset. The dashboard enabled stakeholders to analyze aging balances, outstanding invoices, customer payments, and collection performance through interactive visualizations and KPI reporting.

The solution improved visibility into AR activity and provided financial teams with actionable insights to support collection efforts, cash flow monitoring, and financial decision-making.
