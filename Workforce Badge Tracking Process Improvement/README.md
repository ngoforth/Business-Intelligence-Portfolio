## Project Overview

Designed and implemented a workforce badge tracking and process improvement solution to support employee onboarding, badging, access management, and compliance monitoring activities.

The solution provided stakeholders with centralized visibility into badge application status, background screening progress, site access authorizations, company asset assignments, and employee readiness tracking. Additional processes were established to monitor recurring compliance requirements for employees requiring elevated access credentials and to maintain records of personnel authorized to access specific customer locations.

By consolidating workforce information into a structured process, the solution improved operational transparency, reduced manual tracking efforts, and ensured workforce records remained current and audit-ready.
